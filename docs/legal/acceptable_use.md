# KEYSTONE Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Ownership:** Jordan Maxwell  

## 1. Prohibited Use Cases
You agree not to use KEYSTONE to generate, promote, or distribute:
- Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or exploitation content.
- Malware, ransomware, spyware, or keyloggers.
- Chemical, biological, radiological, or nuclear weapon construction tutorials.
- Non-consensual sexual imagery or fraudulent deepfakes without legal authorization.
- System destruction or exploitation instructions (e.g. destructive SQL or bash injection).

## 2. Enforcement & Aikido Containment
All user inputs are analyzed by SafetyPolicyEvaluator and quarantined within `<untrusted_content>` tags. Deliberate breakout attempts or policy breaches result in immediate job denial (`DENY`), session termination, and potential account suspension.
