## 1. What Are Cookies
Cookies and local storage tokens are small data files stored on your browser to maintain session state and authenticate API requests.

## 2. Cookies We Use

- **Functional Preferences:** Workspace UI configuration, canvas layout preferences, theme selection.
- **Zero Third-Party Advertising Cookies:** KEYSTONE never uses third-party marketing, cross-site trackers, or behavioral advertising cookies.
