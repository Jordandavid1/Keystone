# KEYSTONE Credit & Refund Terms

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Ownership:** Jordan Maxwell  

## 1. Credit Ledger Holds & Settlements
- Creative requests trigger a provisional credit hold (`HOLD`) against your balance.
- If a generation fails technical verification or quality harness checks, the hold is automatically released (`RELEASE`) back to your available balance.
- If generation completes successfully, the hold is finalized as a settlement (`SETTLE`).

## 2. Refund Eligibility
Refunds are issued to the original payment method or as credit ledger additions (`REFUND`) under the following circumstances:
- System outages or prolonged hardware unavailability exceeding SLA commitments.
- Verified billing errors or double charges.
- Catastrophic worker failures where credits were deducted without providing output.
