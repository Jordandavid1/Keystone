# KEYSTONE Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 27, 2026  
**Ownership:** Proprietary software owned by Jordan Maxwell  

## 1. Introduction & Sovereign Data Commitment
KEYSTONE ("we", "us", "our") provides an authoritative prompt-intelligence engine, multimodal reasoning, and generative creative platform. We operate under a sovereign privacy architecture: your personal data and creative assets are strictly isolated within your tenant workspace.

## 2. Personal Information Collected
- **Account Data:** Email address, user identifier, hashed authentication credentials.
- **Billing Records:** Stripe customer identifiers and append-only ledger transaction histories. No raw credit card details are stored on KEYSTONE infrastructure.
- **Creative Inputs:** Prompts, images, video assets, and user feedback.
- **Operational Telemetry:** Execution runtimes, token counts, and quality evaluation scores.

## 3. PII Vault & Zero-Plain-PII Model Invariant
Untrusted user inputs and web scrape data are sanitized via boundary edge-level PII scrubbing. Personal identifiers (names, email addresses, phone numbers) are redacted or tokenized using AES-256-GCM before entering model cognitive contexts.

## 4. GDPR Article 17 ("Right to Erasure")
Users maintain the absolute right to request cascading deletion of their accounts, sessions, assets, and tokenized vault records.
